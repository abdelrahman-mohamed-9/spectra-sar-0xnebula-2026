# SPECTRA-SAR — Team 0xNEBULA

Challenge: Dancing with the SARs | NASA Space Apps Cairo 2026.

SPECTRA-SAR is an exploratory research tool for comparing SAR acquisitions. It validates source metadata, visualizes candidate backscatter differences, queues georeferenced patches for inspection and exports SHA-256 evidence and traceable reports. It helps a human reviewer focus without mistaking a radar difference for confirmed damage.

## Data & scientific status

NASA NISAR GCOV Alaska HH observations from 17 and 29 October 2026 are included as cropped, source-documented samples. Their processing versions differ and the observations are not independently certified for scientific equivalence. A separate Sentinel-1 Spain benchmark with held-out labels illustrates baseline vs experimental ML precision–recall trade-offs. Spain population exposure remains unavailable without a verified matching raster. No validated flood forecast, damage estimate or glacier velocity claim is made.

See `data/nisar/manifest.json`, `data/benchmark-source.json`, `docs/SOURCES.md`, `JUDGE-DEMO-2-MIN-PITCH.md` and `README-ENGINEERING.md` for supporting methods and limitations.
