## Separate scientific model usage

**scikit-learn** supports experimental spatial ML comparisons and evaluation of radar-change candidates. These outputs are not independently validated as flood predictions, building-damage estimates, or disaster-alert decisions. Real observations and their provenance are distinct from AI-generated illustrative media.

## Scientific accountability and media labels

The team is responsible for reviewing code and text produced with AI assistance, confirming factual claims, checking sources and licenses, and clearly distinguishing current prototype capabilities from future research proposals.

If the submitted slides/video include AI-generated pictures, video frames or narration, apply the NASA/Cairo attribution and labeling requirements to the actual media: visible SI-generated labeling for generated imagery/video, and audio acknowledgements in video description/metadata when narration is generated.

Do not claim NASA endorsement or label synthetic or illustrative imagery as an authentic observation. Do not use generated content to reproduce or alter protected NASA branding.
